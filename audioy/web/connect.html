audioY

Version 3.0

This computer's sound, sent to a phone over the local network.

Connect a phone

...

  1. Point the phone's camera at the code and open the link.
  2. Tap once when the page asks. Safari will not play sound otherwise.
  3. Connect the earphones as usual. The phone handles the Bluetooth.
